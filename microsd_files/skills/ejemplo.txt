# Skills of your own for Bug. Copy this folder ("skills") to the microSD card, next to "music" and "books".
# One skill per line:   phrase | another phrase => answer
# - Capitals, accents and punctuation do not matter when you speak.
# - Put several answers with || and Bug picks one at random.
# - {hora} and {fecha} are filled in from the board's clock.
# - Your skills are checked before the built-in ones, so yours win.
# - The answer is said with the free online voice (needs internet); without internet it is only shown on the screen.
# After changing a file, no restart is needed: Bug reads the card each time.

prueba de la tarjeta | skill de prueba => La tarjeta funciona. Hoy es {fecha}.
como esta mi perro => Está muy bien || Está durmiendo || Pidió comida otra vez
a que hora es la reunion => La reunión de equipo es a las diez de la mañana
dime un dato curioso de mi casa => Vives en un lugar con mucha historia
